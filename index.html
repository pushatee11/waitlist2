FAQs Common questions about our Greater Accra rollout.

WhatsApp: +233 24 000 0000 Email: info@dgmwireless.com
When do you launch? +
Trunk line trenching and installation begins in late 2026. Specific neighborhood activation dates are prioritized based on waitlist density.
Does joining cost anything? +
No. Joining the waitlist is 100% free with no deposit, no credit card, and zero obligation.
How much will it cost? +
Pricing will be announced at launch and structured to compete favorably with existing broadband providers, while offering superior fiber speeds. Early members receive locked-in founder rates.
Is data really unlimited? +
Yes. Pure fiber lines have true unlimited throughput. There are no fair usage caps, no peak-hour slowdowns, and no throttling.
Which areas will you cover? +
We are deploying across Greater Accra, including East Legon, Spintex, Tema, Ashaiman, Madina, Adenta, Osu, Cantonments, Labadi, Teshie, Kasoa, Dansoman, North Legon, Haatso, Taifa, Dome, Pokuase, Amasaman, and surrounding communities.
What do you do with my details? +
Your details are stored securely and used solely to calculate neighborhood demand and notify you when fiber is ready for installation on your street. We never sell data or send spam.
