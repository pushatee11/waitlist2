When do you launch? +
Infrastructure rollout begins in late 2026. Specific neighborhood activation timelines are dictated by registration density on this waitlist.
Does joining cost anything? +
No. Joining the waitlist is 100% free with no deposit, no credit card, and zero obligation.
How much will it cost? +
Pricing will be announced at launch and structured to compete favorably with existing mobile and broadband providers, while offering superior fiber speeds. Waitlist members receive guaranteed founder rates.
Is data really unlimited? +
Yes. Pure fiber lines have true unlimited throughput. There are no fair usage caps, no peak-hour limits, and no throttling.
Which areas will you cover? +
Our network backbone spans Greater Accra, including East Legon, Spintex, Tema, Osu, Cantonments, Madina, Adenta, Dansoman, and neighboring communities. Deployment order is determined directly by demand.
What do you do with my details? +
Your details are stored securely and used exclusively to map neighborhood interest and send you launch notifications. We never share or sell your data.
